## What it demonstrates

Problem to solve: Our agency has several existing live products that were managed as projects over the years. Because these products need to be managed and updated, the PM needs to know, from existing documentation and meetings, what key product management information exists about each product (problem statement, business hypothesis, user testing, value proposition, competitive analysis, market positioning, user acquisition strategy, etc.). The prototype is meant to be a dashboard that helps the product team, the PMs, and the team manager have an idea of the current status of a product in terms of product management to know what to work on next. When information is missing, for example a problem statement, the product should provide a suggestion based on existing documentation, emails and meeting transcripts, then indicate that it is a suggestion, and add this as a next step for the product.
